About SPHEREx

SPHEREx is a NASA space telescope mission. It observes the entire sky in 102 different near-infrared wavelength bands. It helps scientists study galaxies, stars, cosmic history, and other objects in the universe. This website lets users compare two space observation images and visualize possible movement between two positions.
